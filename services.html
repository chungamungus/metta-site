Frequently Asked Questions

How do private dinners work?

I bring the restaurant to you: your home, your Airbnb, your family’s lake house. We’ll plan the menu together, or you can hand it all to me and be a guest at your own party.

  • Dinners for 4–20 guests (larger events are sometimes possible, just ask)
  • Full service: planning, shopping, cooking, course-by-course service, and cleanup
  • I arrive about 2–3 hours before your dinner to set up and prep
What does meal prep involve?

Heads up: I’m not taking new meal prep clients right now. Current clients are unaffected, and you’re welcome to join the waitlist.

Customized menus prepared in your kitchen for the week ahead, built around your household’s tastes, dietary needs, and schedule, so you eat well on weeknights without cooking every day.

  • Weekly, biweekly, or whenever you need it
  • Four to five dinners for your household, made with fresh, high-quality ingredients
  • Everything packed, labeled, and organized in your fridge or freezer
  • Optional add-ons: specialty sides, breakfasts, snacks, or desserts
What is your service area?

I’m based in Central Massachusetts and primarily serve Central MA, MetroWest, and Greater Boston. I also travel throughout Southern New England (the Berkshires, Cape Cod, coastal Connecticut and Rhode Island) for select events, with a travel fee added to the quote.

How do I book?

Fill out the inquiry form with your date, headcount, location, and any menu ideas. I’ll follow up with availability and a quote, we’ll talk through the menu, and a deposit locks in your date.

How does payment work?

Invoices are sent by email through Square. A $200 deposit reserves your date and is applied to your total; it’s fully refundable up to 7 days before the event. The balance is due on the day of the event. I accept credit cards, Venmo, Zelle, and cash.

How are ingredients billed?

Separately, and at cost. Your quote includes an ingredient estimate based on the menu; after I shop, I adjust the final invoice to the actual receipt. You’re never paying a markup on groceries.

What types of events do you cook for?
  • Birthday and anniversary dinners
  • Date nights at home
  • Dinner parties and holiday meals (Thanksgiving, Christmas, Easter, and more)
  • Backyard weddings, rehearsal dinners, and elopements
  • Bachelorette weekends and Galentine’s dinners
  • Wellness retreats and corporate retreats
  • Family reunions and vacation-rental getaways
  • Beer, wine, and cocktail pairing dinners
  • Or whatever else you can dream up
Do I need to provide a kitchen?

Yes. As long as you have a working oven or stovetop and some counter space, we’re all set. Everything is finished on-site in your kitchen (some prep may happen ahead of time in a commercial kitchen). I bring my own professional cooking equipment, and I can supply plates, tableware, and glassware on request.

Do I need to be there while you cook?

Not at all. I’m happy to work independently while you enjoy your guests, but you’re welcome to hang out in the kitchen. I love chatting with my clients. For meal prep, a brief conversation before the first session helps me get your household right.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. Gluten-free, vegetarian, vegan, dairy-free, nut allergies, kosher-style, low-FODMAP: I build menus around allergies and preferences so every guest at the table gets a full experience, not a sad substitute. Just let me know when you inquire.

Do you provide alcohol?

I don’t sell or serve alcohol due to licensing restrictions. I do build pairings, and you provide the bottles. Craft beer is my specialty, and I’m happy to suggest wine, cocktails, or mocktails course by course. For full bar service, I can coordinate with a licensed mobile bartending service.

Are you insured and certified?

Yes. I carry liability insurance through the Food Liability Insurance Program (FLIP), I’m a certified ServSafe® Food Protection Manager, and I hold a current Allergen Awareness Certificate.

What about cleanup?

All mine. Your kitchen will be cleaner than I found it, and any leftovers go home with you in labeled containers.

How far in advance should I book?

For private dinners, 2–3 weeks is ideal, and more for holidays and peak summer weekends. Shorter notice is sometimes possible, so it never hurts to ask. Meal prep clients schedule recurring sessions based on availability.

Is there a minimum, and do holidays cost more?

Yes on both. Dinners start at $650, which covers up to five guests at the plated rate; above that, pricing is per person. Holiday dates (Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, New Year’s Eve, and Valentine’s Day) book early and are priced at a premium, so reach out as soon as you know your date.

Do you offer gift certificates?

Yes. A private dinner makes a memorable gift for birthdays, anniversaries, new parents, and holidays. Get in touch and I’ll set one up.

What’s the difference between a personal chef and a caterer?

Catering usually means food prepared in bulk off-site and dropped off or set up buffet-style; it’s the right call for big events. A personal chef dinner is smaller, more personal, and more of an experience: I write a menu just for your group, cook it fresh in your kitchen, and serve it course by course. If the food and the evening are the point, that’s what I do.
