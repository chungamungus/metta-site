About Jeff

Meet Chef Jeff Raymond

I started Metta Personal Chef because I believe a great meal is about more than what’s on the plate. It’s about the care behind it and the people gathered around the table.

Before cooking full-time, my path ran through professional brewing, baking, and a background in chemistry and food science. I bring all of that into the kitchen: a precise, technical approach to the fundamentals, paired with a real love of playing with flavor. My cooking leans on New England’s seasons and the Mediterranean: bright, fresh, and generous. Every menu is written for the specific group I’m cooking for.

At the end of the day, my goal is simple: make your event delicious and effortless, so all you have to do is enjoy it. I can’t wait to cook for you.

Mettā [noun]

A term in Buddhism often translated as “loving-kindness.” It is a heartfelt wish for the welfare and happiness of others, and a meditation practice for cultivating that goodwill.

I try to embody metta in everything I do, and the kitchen is where it comes most naturally. For me, a beautifully cooked meal and genuine hospitality are simply how I express metta.
