Dinner out, without going out

Hi, I’m Jeff Raymond. I’m a personal chef based in Central Mass, and I cook seasonal, ingredient-driven meals in homes, vacation rentals, and retreat spaces with genuine care and hospitality.

Every menu is written from scratch for your group, around the season, your tastes, and any dietary needs at the table. I do the shopping, the cooking, and the serving, and I leave your kitchen cleaner than I found it. All you have to do is enjoy your guests!

1

Tell me about your event

Date, headcount, location, and favorite dishes. The inquiry form takes two minutes.

2

Get a quote and a custom menu

I’ll send pricing and a first draft of your menu, and we’ll fine-tune it together until it’s exactly right.

3

Sit back and enjoy

I arrive a couple of hours early, cook, serve each course, and clean up. Leftovers get packed and labeled for you.
